How quickly can you respond to a Macon rodent call?

Same-day inspections are standard for active infestations across Bibb County. We're open 24/7 — calls placed before 9 a.m. typically get an inspection that same afternoon. Outer-county addresses (Warner Robins, Forsyth, Milledgeville) may run a day longer in peak season.

Are your treatments safe around pets and kids?

Yes. We use tamper-resistant bait stations for any rodenticide work, placed in locations pets and children can't access. For interior infestations we lean on snap traps and exclusion rather than poison — it's faster, cleaner, and removes the wall-decomposition risk.

Why do older Vineville and Ingleside homes get more roof rats?

Two reasons. The mature pecan and live-oak canopy throughout those neighborhoods gives roof rats direct attic access via overhanging branches. And the early-1900s housing stock typically has wood-shingle vents, soffit gaps, and unfinished knee-wall cavities that act as ready-made rat highways once they're in.
