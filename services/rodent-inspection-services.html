What the inspection covers, five zones

Zone 1: Roofline

Ridge vents, soffit returns, gable vents (east and west faces), dormer flashing, roofline utility penetrations, canopy access branches within reach of the roofline. Primary zone for roof rat check in Vineville, Ingleside, and Shirley Hills.

Zone 2: Attic interior

Attic entry and interior inspection, dropping distribution, runway grease marks along joists, nesting evidence, insulation condition, daylight gaps at the roof deck perimeter. Confirms whether roofline vulnerabilities have been exploited.

Zone 3: Crawl space

Interior crawl space entry, foundation vent screens, mudsill-to-foundation gaps, pipe penetrations on the interior face, activity evidence (runways, droppings, nesting). Exterior-only check misses the most major Norway rat entry points.

We didn't sell a treatment, the value was in the documentation.

Zone 4: Foundation perimeter

Exterior perimeter walk, burrow activity near the foundation, below-grade utility entries, garage door threshold gaps, visible foundation vent condition, downspout termination gaps.

Zone 5: Interior living space

Under-sink inspection in kitchen and bathrooms, behind appliances, wall-floor junctions in kitchen and utility rooms, garage interior. Confirms interior evidence and helps localize infestation to specific areas of the home.

What the written report includes

Every inspection generates a written report in PDF format. The report covers:

  • Inspection date and property address
  • Description of each zone inspected and findings by zone
  • Species identification with supporting evidence (dropping size, runway location, entry-point height, activity pattern)
  • Numbered entry-point list with location description, approximate gap size, and recommended seal material for each gap identified
  • Treatment recommendation: removal approach (snap trap program, visit frequency), exclusion scope (which zones need sealing), and cleanup recommendation if contamination was found
  • Canopy trimming recommendations for properties with roofline-accessible canopy

The report accurately reflects what was found, no fabricated clearances, no inflated infestation severity to justify a larger treatment scope. If no active infestation is found, the report documents that along with any entry-point vulnerabilities that warrant preventive attention.

Frequently asked questions, rodent inspection in Macon

Is the rodent inspection really free in Macon?

Yes. Full property inspection with written report, five zones, species confirmation, entry-point map, treatment recommendation, is free with no obligation to proceed. We charge for treatment, exclusion, and cleanup work. We don't charge for the inspection that scopes that work.

What does a rodent inspection cover in Macon?

Five zones: roofline (ridge, soffit, gable, dormer, canopy); attic interior; crawl space interior (entered, not just exterior check); foundation perimeter exterior; interior living space (under sinks, behind appliances, kitchen and utility room wall junctions). Written report covers all zones with entry-point locations and recommended seal materials.

How long does a rodent inspection take in Macon?

30 to 90 minutes. A newer home with limited inventory runs 30–45 minutes. A pre-1920s bungalow with full crawl space, mature canopy, and possible attic activity runs 60–90 minutes. We don't rush the inspection.

When should I schedule a rodent inspection in Macon?

When you've noticed evidence (droppings, gnaw marks, noise, sighting); for a real estate transaction; to relist an Airbnb after a guest complaint; for annual preventive check in high-pressure neighborhoods; or to verify prior exclusion work is still holding.

What does the written inspection report include?

Inspection date and address; findings by zone; species identification with supporting evidence; numbered entry-point list with location, gap size, and recommended seal material; treatment recommendation covering removal, exclusion, and cleanup scope; canopy trimming recommendations where applicable.

Can I use the inspection report for a real estate transaction?

Yes. The report documents rodent status in a format suitable for disclosure purposes and transaction records. It accurately reflects findings, no fabricated clearances, no inflated severity.

What's the difference between a rodent inspection and a general pest inspection?

General WDI inspections focus on wood-destroying insects. Our inspections are rodent-specific, more thorough in the crawl space and attic zones where most Macon rodent evidence is concentrated, and calibrated for the Norway rat, roof rat, and house mouse species present in Bibb County.

How quickly can you schedule an inspection in Macon?

Same-day inspection is available for most Bibb County addresses, calls before 9 a.m. get afternoon slots. Non-emergency inspections (pre-listing, preventive, annual) are usually scheduled within 1–3 business days. Call (844) 635-0403 for a confirmed window.

What should I prepare before the inspection?

Clear access to under-sink areas, behind appliances, garage, and the attic hatch if you have one. Note where you've noticed activity so the inspector can give extra attention to those areas. Don't clean up droppings before the inspection, the distribution pattern is important evidence for species identification.
