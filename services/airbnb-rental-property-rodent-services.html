The Cherry Blossom Festival rodent window, why March is different

Cherry Blossom Festival usually runs in mid-March and draws its largest crowds to Macon's historic Vineville, Ingleside, and Shirley Hills neighborhoods, precisely because those neighborhoods are the most picturesque and walkable in the city. The same neighborhoods have the densest roof rat pressure in Macon, driven by the same mature pecan-and-live-oak canopy that makes them visually distinctive.

The timing creates a predictable collision. Mid-March is when Bibb County roof rats are at peak late-winter attic activity. They're shifting from winter nesting to spring foraging. Attic activity is highest. Evidence of rodents in the home's living areas peaks. And short-term rental demand for those historic homes hits its yearly maximum. The combination produces Macon's highest short-term rental rodent call volume of the year. Every year. Reliably.

The prevention program is simple: a full inspection in February, exclusion sealing of any identified entry points, and a pre-Festival walkthrough to confirm no new activity. The reactive program, calling the week before the Festival after a guest pre-arrival inspection finds evidence, is expensive, stressful, and doesn't guarantee clearance before the first guest arrives. February inspection is the right call.

Frequently asked questions, short-term rental rodent services in Macon

What rodent services do short-term rental owners in Macon most commonly need?

Pre-listing inspections, urgent clearance when a guest reports activity, and pre-season programs before Cherry Blossom Festival and summer peak. Written records is required for all three.

How quickly can you respond to a short-term rental situation in Macon?

Same-day inspection is standard across Bibb County. Written clearance report on the same visit if no active infestation is found. If rodents are present, written inspection report and treatment scope provided on-site same day.

What records do you provide for Airbnb hosts?

Written inspection report with: inspection date, property address, areas inspected, what was found, any treatment performed, and entry-point recommendations. Suitable for Airbnb support, guests, or dispute resolution. We don't fabricate clearance, the report accurately reflects what was found.

Can rodent activity cause my Airbnb listing to be suspended?

Yes. Guest reports of rodent activity are a common trigger for Airbnb safety-related listing holds. recorded pro treatment and written clearance are usually what Airbnb requires for reinstatement. We provide this records routinely.

When should I schedule a pre-season inspection for Cherry Blossom Festival?

No later than late February, ideally early February. Roof rats in Vineville, Ingleside, and Shirley Hills finish their winter nesting cycle right at the Festival window. If treatment is needed, it takes 2–3 weeks to complete. Don't schedule the week before the event.

A guest just texted me about a mouse in my Macon rental. What do I do?

we can help immediately. Ask the guest to document what they saw. We give you a same-day window and provide a same-day snap trap visit plus a written report for your guest communication and platform records.

How do I prevent rodent problems before Cherry Blossom Festival?

February inspection, exclusion sealing of identified entry points, and a pre-Festival walkthrough within 48 hours of the first arrival date. Properties in Vineville, Ingleside, and Shirley Hills should do this annually, not just when they've seen evidence.

How much does inspection and clearance cost for a Macon short-term rental?

Standalone inspection is free. Treatment, if needed, is scoped and quoted on-site. Pre-season prevention programs (inspection plus exclusion) run $300 to $800 depending on property size and entry points identified.

Is it my responsibility as the host to address rodent problems?

Yes. Rodents entering through structural gaps are a building-upkeep issue, not a guest-created condition. Asking guests to handle it escalates the situation to a negative review and platform complaint. Call a pro immediately and provide recorded pro treatment.
